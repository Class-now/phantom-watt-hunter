Hunt down phantom loads in your room

Chargers, consoles, TVs and keep-warm heaters draw power even when you think they're off. This walkthrough goes room by room, estimates each device's standby use per year in kWh, money and CO₂ using published typical values, and turns the fixes you pick into a checklist you can track.

Every number is shown as a range, because standby power varies a lot between models. If you have a plug-in power meter, enter your measured watts and the range shrinks to the meter's accuracy.

Sources & assumptions
  • Yearly kWh = watts × hours per day in standby × 365 ÷ 1000 × number of devices.
  • Each device uses a low / typical / high watt range rounded from the sources below. The headline is the typical value; the range is what an unusually frugal or unusually hungry model would give.
  • A measured value from your own plug meter replaces the range with ±10%.
  • Smart plugs cost their own 0.3–1 W, 24 h a day; that is subtracted from their savings. Switched power strips are assumed to use nothing.
  • Manual habits are scaled by your follow-through slider (default 80%).
  • Money and CO₂ are kWh × your price and grid factor. Grid factors are annual averages, not the marginal plant your saving actually turns down.
